Questions owners ask

Do I need a computer?

No. You can set up everything from your phone.

Does it work in Swahili?

Yes. The site and chatbot reply in English or Swahili, and we are testing Sheng with salon owners now.

How do I get paid?

We are building M-Pesa payment links so customers can pay you directly. You will be able to use your own till or paybill.

What if the chatbot does not know the answer?

It says so and passes the question to you, so a customer never gets a made-up price.

How much will it cost?

Founding members get free setup and the pilot. We will share monthly prices before anything is charged.
